The Problem
What Today's Mobile World Gets Wrong
Consumer Side

Pervasive Data Exploitation

Your phone tracks everywhere you go and sells that data for profit. 42% of apps request cross-app tracking. Most users have no idea it is happening.

$278B data broker market · 42% of apps track across devices

Forced Upgrade Cycles

Phones are deliberately slowed down and stripped of updates after a few years, pushing consumers into costly replacements before the device is truly worn out.

62M tonnes e-waste in 2022 · only 22% recycled

A Growing E-Waste Crisis

E-waste rises by 2.6 million tonnes every year and is projected to hit 82 million tonnes by 2030. Only 1% of rare earth elements are recovered from discarded devices.

$37B annual economic loss · UN Global E-waste Monitor

Opaque, Unethical Supply Chains

An estimated 40,000 children work in cobalt mining tied to lithium-ion battery supply. Consumers have no way to verify what is inside the device they carry every day.

20% of DRC cobalt from artisanal mines · UNICEF data

The Problem
The Business Risk Is Equally Serious
Business Side

Employee Device Data Leaks

48% of organizations suffered a breach linked to unmanaged personal devices last year. When staff handle client work on personal phones, businesses lose visibility and take on real liability.

VMI 2025 · 46% of breach credentials from BYOD machines

Compliance and Audit Exposure

The average US data breach costs $9.36 million. Consumer-grade phones create audit blind spots. Once a regulator asks what was accessed, standard hardware cannot answer.

IBM 2024 · Global average breach = $4.88M

Executive Targeting

SIM swap fraud rose 1,055% in 2024 year over year. Mobile phishing is increasingly personalized. The phone is often the weakest security link in any organization.

1,055% SIM swap increase YoY · 2025 security reports

Insurance and Risk Premiums

Cyber insurers flag BYOD environments as coverage gaps. Weak mobile security raises premiums, tightens underwriting limits, and can reduce insurability after a serious incident.

MFA prevents up to 90% of attacks · underwriting guidance

The Solution

Ground-up custom OS. Blocks 5,000+ daily data points at the source. Short-lived permissions auto-expire. Work and personal data fully isolated. Compliance logs generated automatically.

Privacy by Default

No tracking without consent. Permissions expire automatically. Minimal data from first boot.

Nyro Solar Power System

Repair-First Hardware

Upgrade and Return Model
